# Gunu Digital — Business Ready

## Upload
Upload `index.html`, `hero.png`, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` to Hostinger `public_html`.

## Before going live
1. Replace `919999999999` in `index.html` with the real WhatsApp number.
2. Replace every `G-XXXXXXXXXX` with the Google Analytics 4 Measurement ID.
3. Keep the canonical/domain as `https://gunudigital.in/` if that is your final domain; otherwise replace it with the real domain.
4. Enable HTTPS/SSL in hosting.

## Google Analytics
Create a GA4 web data stream for the domain and paste the Measurement ID into `G-XXXXXXXXXX`.

Tracked events:
- `whatsapp_click`
- `phone_click`
- `email_click`
- `generate_lead`

## Google Search Console
Add `https://gunudigital.in/`, verify ownership, and submit:
`https://gunudigital.in/sitemap.xml`

Then use URL Inspection for the homepage after publishing.

## SEO
The page includes SEO title/description, canonical, robots directives, Open Graph metadata and LocalBusiness structured data. It also contains relevant natural service terms for Jaipur. Do not keyword-stuff or claim official government affiliation.

## Important
Google Analytics/Search Console cannot guarantee first-place Google rankings. They provide measurement, indexing and search-performance tools.
